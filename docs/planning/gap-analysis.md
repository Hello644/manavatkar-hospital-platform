# Completeness Critique — v1 Hospital Platform Drafts

## A) GAPS (prioritized)

1. **Server-down-during-OPD operations is unowned.** Arch gives a warm standby + "swap DNS," but: WHO flips it (no on-site IT; Tailscale remote support fails if internet is also down), a one-command/laminated failover runbook executable by the admin, a paper degraded-mode kit (pre-printed token pads, Rx pads, manual attendance sheet) with a defined **back-entry workflow** after recovery, and — completely missing — the **fail-back/merge story**: after running on a ≤24h-stale standby, visits/Rx/punches entered on the standby diverge from the recovered primary. No draft addresses reconciliation.
2. **Paper-record and opening-data migration.** Nothing on: existing patient case files (policy needed: bulk import vs "re-register on next visit" with old-file-number cross-reference field), existing appointment diary cutover, **leave-balance opening balances as of go-live** (the leave ledger cannot compute without them), and partial-month attendance if attendance goes live mid-payroll-cycle.
3. **Staff-facing UI language undecided.** Drafts localize Rx instructions, TTS, and consent notices, but the reception/nurse UI language (English-only? Hindi/Marathi labels?) is never chosen. Django i18n scaffolding is cheap day-1, expensive retrofit. Related unstated decision: patient names stored Latin-transliterated only vs Devanagari — this determines whether pg_trgm search works and must be fixed before the registry ships.
4. **No consolidated rollout/training/parallel-run plan.** Compliance mentions a 2-week attendance parallel run and month-1 paper fallback for Rx; arch mentions "training materials" in phase 4. Missing: per-module cutover sequence and go-live/exit criteria, a training/sandbox instance with dummy patients, named super-users per shift, typing-proficiency assessment of desk staff, and payroll sign-off gate before the register replaces the physical one.
5. **MLC workflow absent from the clinical draft.** Compliance specs an `is_mlc` flag; the OPD/registration flow has no capture point: MLC flag at registration/visit, quick-registration for unknown/unconscious patients ("UNKNOWN male ~40"), brought-by/police-station/injury fields, "MLC" watermark on prints, WhatsApp-share block for MLC documents, and the 10-year retention hook.
6. **Attendance edge cases not modeled:** shift **swaps** between nurses (ubiquitous; needs a swap-edit or approval flow, else every swap becomes a manual correction), split shifts (two work intervals/day breaks the single IN/OUT-window inference in attendance §5c/§6), double-shift/OT continuation punches, **visiting consultants/part-timers** (punch-exempt category or per-visit honorarium log — they distort absentee dashboards otherwise), staff called in off-roster at night (OD/emergency status), and multi-entrance policy (single kiosk assumed; back/emergency doors unaddressed beyond the phone-PWA aside).
7. **No visit disposition.** This is a 10–50 **bed** hospital; the consult screen ends at Print & Next with only a referral note. Needs disposition enum (home/review/**advise admission**/refer-out/expired) so OPD→IPD handoff and outcomes exist in the record even with IPD out of scope.
8. **Printing dies in power cuts.** UPS/inverter plans cover server, switch, kiosk, reception PC — but laser printers (300–800 W peak) and doctor-room PCs are not assigned to inverter circuits. Mid-consultation cut = no Rx/token output despite autosave. Specify inverter-circuit outlets per room or the paper-pad fallback + reprint-later procedure.
9. **Doctor-absence handling.** No flow for a doctor cancelling a day: bulk cancel/reschedule of booked appointments, front-desk call list, redirecting the live queue to another doctor, refund handling beyond a flag.
10. **Shared-phone consequences half-handled.** Family linking exists, but: duplicate detection keyed on exact mobile will false-positive heavily in shared-phone households (tune/branch the logic); **WhatsApp Rx to the family head's number is a confidentiality leak for adult patients** — require per-share number confirmation + recorded consent; follow-up SMS to shared phones names the patient.
11. **Queue-display privacy never stated as a rule.** The display spec happens to show tokens only — make "token numbers only, no names/diagnosis on public display or TTS" an explicit requirement, and add front-desk screen positioning (search results expose other patients' name/age/area/last-visit to whoever is at the counter).
12. **Seasonal surge (monsoon/dengue, 2–3× OPD) unaddressed:** per-doctor daily token caps, wait-estimate degradation, a second registration terminal option, fast onboarding of temporary staff into attendance (locum *doctor* accounts exist; temp *staff* enrollment doesn't), and a "camp day" mode.
13. **Browser TTS is an offline trap.** Web Speech API hi-IN/mr-IN voices on cheap Android TV sticks typically call Google's network TTS — announcements silently fail offline. Pre-generate MP3 snippets server-side (token numbers are a small finite set) instead.
14. **Clock integrity offline.** Compliance demands NTP-synced audit timestamps and clock-anomaly logging, but no draft specs it: server as sole timestamp authority, chrony with local RTC fallback during multi-day outages, reconciliation rule for kiosk store-and-forward punches carrying device time, drift alarm.
15. **Money reconciliation minimally specced.** Consult fee + mode is recorded, but no receipt-number series, daily collection register (cash/UPI split) for the owner, refund workflow, or doctor-wise revenue/OPD MIS — the first report a proprietor-doctor will ask for.
16. **Post-launch support model.** Who receives `/healthz`/backup-age alerts, AMC/on-call terms after week 16, spare-hardware custody, and ongoing ownership of formulary additions and roster admin after handover.
17. **Every-staff login model.** Leave requests via emp_code+PIN imply ~120 self-service accounts; arch's role list has no `staff` role, and no PIN/password reset flow for low-literacy users.
18. **Lab-results loop open.** Investigations print as a checklist but results never re-enter the record; state the v1 position (explicit exclusion vs. a photo/scan attachment on the visit).

## B) CONFLICTS (prioritized)

1. **Scanned-signature & WhatsApp legality:** clinical §4 offers a per-doctor opt-in to paste a scanned signature image on PDFs and ships wa.me PDF sharing day one; compliance §3 states a scanned/typed signature image is **not valid under the IT Act** and that WhatsApp sharing must be gated on Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020. Incompatible defaults on a day-one feature — the opt-in toggle and the share flow's legal posture must be resolved (drop the toggle, or brand shared PDFs as unsigned copies per clinical's own footer approach).
2. **Biometric templates in cloud backup:** attendance §8 says "ensure biometric tables are included in encryption scope" (i.e., backed up); compliance §1 says "ideally exclude raw templates from cloud backup entirely and re-enroll after disaster." Direct fork with DR consequences (exclusion = re-enrolling ~120 staff after any bare-metal restore).
3. **PDF engine and silent printing:** clinical §5 mandates headless Chromium/Puppeteer + SumatraPDF print-agent per room PC; arch §6 mandates WeasyPrint + Chrome `--kiosk-printing`. Same pipeline, two different stacks each.
4. **Registration flow vs consent duties:** compliance §1 requires a privacy-notice checkbox at registration and **guardian name + relationship for minors** in v1; clinical §1.1's field list contains neither, and its <60 s registration target doesn't budget for them.
5. **Face-recognition parameters diverge across the board:** threshold 0.42 + top1−top2 ≥ 0.08 margin + three-band (attendance §4) vs "~0.45–0.5, tune" with no margin rule (arch §3); enrollment 5–8 samples, gallery cap 12 with adaptive update (attendance §1) vs 3–5 photos (arch §3); matching in numpy with explicit "no vector DB" (attendance §2) vs pgvector (arch §1/§3); detector SCRFD-500MF (attendance) vs buffalo_l's SCRFD-10G (arch). Attendance's spec is the more complete; arch must inherit it.
6. **Read-access audit is required but the chosen tool can't do it:** compliance §4 mandates logging "every view of a patient's clinical history"; arch §7's `django-auditlog` captures create/update/delete diffs only — view logging needs custom middleware nobody has scoped.
7. **Queue-update transport:** clinical §2.2 promises WebSocket updates <2 s plus auto-call-next; arch §1 prescribes HTMX polling/SSE every 3–5 s, Redis deferred, "websockets only if/when." Arch's choice undercuts clinical's stated UX numbers.
8. **UHID and scan symbology:** clinical §1.2 specifies `RHM-26-004217-3` (3-letter + YY + 6-digit + Luhn, Code-128 barcode); arch §1 specifies `HSP-2026-000123` and QR-of-visit-ID on thermal tokens. Shared-registry key format and scanner choice must be one scheme.
9. **Punch-photo retention:** 30-day hard-delete (attendance §8) vs 90 days (arch §3) vs "30–90" (compliance §4). Pick one number; it appears in the consent notice.
10. **Kiosk offline queue tech:** attendance §9 says local **SQLite** queue (native-app assumption, and leaves "Android app or PWA" open); arch §2 fixes PWA-in-Fully-Kiosk with **IndexedDB + service worker**. Same feature, incompatible implementations.
11. **Liveness strategy:** compliance risk #10 recommends blink/motion liveness and commercial SDKs (Luxand/FaceIO, ML Kit); attendance §3 and arch §3 both explicitly reject active blink and commercial SDKs in favor of passive MiniFASNet + policy deterrents.
12. **Attendance data model:** attendance §5 mandates storing raw direction-less punch events with IN/OUT as a re-runnable server-side interpretation; arch §1's `AttendanceEvent` bakes `direction in/out` into the stored row.
13. **Formulary seeding:** clinical §6 seeds from NLEM 2022 + Jan Aushadhi CSV imports before pharmacist mapping; arch phase 2 asserts "no reliable free national drug DB" and plans pure manual xlsx entry.
14. **Minor hardware mismatches:** kiosk camera height 1.40 m (attendance §9) vs 1.5–1.6 m (arch §2); server RAM "16 GB covers both workloads" (attendance §9) vs 32 GB spec (arch §4); tablet model/price bands differ trivially.
